# Responsible Testing Principles

When conducting authorised security testing:

1. Obtain permission before testing.
2. Clearly define the scope.
3. Test only assets included within that scope.
4. Minimise disruption.
5. Protect information obtained during testing.
6. Do not retain unnecessary sensitive information.
7. Report vulnerabilities responsibly.
8. Stop testing if unexpected risk or damage occurs.
9. Follow the rules established by the system owner or testing programme.
10. Document your authorisation where appropriate.
